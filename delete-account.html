Delete Your Plot & Place: Tiny Town Account

If you would like to delete your Plot & Place: Tiny Town account and the associated personal data, you can request deletion by contacting us at:

Email: plotandplacegame@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your Plot & Place account.

What will be deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, your Plot & Place account and personal data associated with that account will be deleted.

Some transaction records may be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or security purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Plot & Place account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through Google Play.
